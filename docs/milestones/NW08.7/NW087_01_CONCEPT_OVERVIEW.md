# NW08.7 · 01 · Concept overview

NW08.7 explores a signature, scroll-controlled motion experience for the Nextwise homepage. It does **not** change the homepage. Three working prototypes live in a development-only Motion Lab:

| Route | What it is |
|---|---|
| `/motion-lab` | Comparison gallery: idea, storyboard, technique, strengths and risks of each concept, side by side |
| `/motion-lab/living-path` | Concept A, The Living Path |
| `/motion-lab/unfolding-future` | Concept B, The Unfolding Future |
| `/motion-lab/window-to-tomorrow` | Concept C, The Window to Tomorrow |

Append `?motion=reduced` to any concept URL to see its reduced-motion version without changing OS settings.

All three tell the same four-stage story, in the order the brief requires:

1. **What comes next?** Every future starts with a question.
2. **Your future has possibilities.** Interests, strengths and choices you haven't explored yet.
3. **Explore the paths ahead.** Career Intelligence for guided planning in India. Study Abroad across six European countries.
4. **Know What's Next.** Start with the path that fits you.

Each ends at the same real entry points: the five product items from `MAIN_NAV` and the six approved Study Abroad destinations (Germany, Ireland, Netherlands, France, Sweden, Denmark). No statistics, counts, partnerships or results appear anywhere.

## A · The Living Path

One ribbon draws the journey. A point appears, a fine line travels and thickens into a dimensional ribbon. At the fold it branches: Career Intelligence rises to the upper right as an N-arrow trajectory, and Study Abroad runs outward. The official Nextwise mark appears at the arrow tip. Built from one inline SVG with no images.

## B · The Unfolding Future

A small folded sheet floats in white space, turns to face the viewer, and opens fold by fold. Each fold carries one real entry point: Explore Careers, Career Intelligence, Universities and Courses, and Study Abroad with its six countries. Fully open, the sheet *is* the navigation, with working links. Built with CSS 3D transforms on four nested panels. On phones it unfolds vertically.

## C · The Window to Tomorrow

An arched window in a plain wall glows with soft light, which clears to reveal a real campus photograph already in the repository. The viewer steps through the arch into an open horizon where six destination beams rise, one per country. The scene then quietens behind a panel of real entry points. Built from one existing photograph and CSS-only scenery.

See `NW087_02_STORYBOARDS.md` for the frame-by-frame storyboards and `NW087_10_RECOMMENDATION.md` for the recommended concept.
